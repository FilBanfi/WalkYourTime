## 1. Overview

WalkYourTime is a privacy-first application. All data generated and used by the app is stored exclusively on your device. No data is collected, transmitted, or shared with any external server or third party by FilBanfi.

Because WalkYourTime operates entirely offline and FilBanfi has no access to or control over your hardware, **you (the end user) act as the sole Data Controller** of the information generated by the app under applicable privacy laws (such as the GDPR). FilBanfi acts strictly as the software developer and tool provider, bearing no responsibility for data stored locally on your physical device.

---

## 2. Data Collected and Stored

WalkYourTime stores the following data **locally on your device only**, using Android's standard SharedPreferences and/or Room database:

- **Step count data** — recorded primarily via the device's built-in step counter sensor. Alternatively, you may *optionally* choose to retrieve this data locally from **Google Health Connect**. The app functions fully and independently using only the built-in sensor; external integrations are never required.
- **Screen time and app usage data** — monitored via Android Accessibility Service to enforce app blocking rules.
- **User preferences and settings** — including goals, thresholds, and app configuration.

This data never leaves your device and is never accessible to FilBanfi or any third party.

---

## 3. Permissions Used

WalkYourTime requests the following Android permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Accessibility Service** | Monitors foreground app usage to apply screen time limits based on your step count. |
| **Activity Recognition / Step Counter** | Counts your daily steps to calculate earned screen time. |
| **Post Notifications** | Sends local reminders and alerts about your screen time status. |
| **Request Ignore Battery Optimizations** | Ensures the step counter and blocking service continue to work reliably in the background. |
| **Health Connect (Read Steps)** | *Optional.* Reads step count data locally from Google Health Connect to calculate earned screen time, strictly if you choose to enable this integration. The app works perfectly without this permission using the built-in sensor. |

No permission is used for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond the core app functionality described above.

---

## 4. Third-Party Integrations

WalkYourTime is designed to function fully and independently using your device's built-in sensors. However, it offers **optional** integrations with external health platforms to retrieve step count data if you prefer:

- **Google Health Connect (Currently Available):** You may optionally grant WalkYourTime permission to read step data locally from Health Connect. For more information on how Google handles this data, please refer to the [Google Health Connect Privacy Policy](https://health.google/privacy/).
- **Future Integrations (e.g., Huawei Health, Garmin Connect):** Future versions may offer similar optional integrations with other health and fitness services.

For all such integrations, the following privacy principles strictly apply:
- **Strictly Opt-in:** All external integrations are disabled by default. You must explicitly grant WalkYourTime permission via your device's system settings. The app functions perfectly without them.
- **Local Processing Only:** All data exchange happens exclusively on your device. WalkYourTime only reads the specific step count data you authorize. FilBanfi does not write data to these services, nor does it transmit any health data to external servers.
- **No Developer Access:** FilBanfi has absolutely no access to, or control over, any data exchanged between WalkYourTime and third-party health services.
- **Governed by Respective Policies:** Your use of these third-party services is subject to their respective Privacy Policies. WalkYourTime does not share your data with them beyond the local API requests required to fetch your step count.

FilBanfi does **not** sell, rent, share, or disclose any user data to third parties. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting services included in the app.
