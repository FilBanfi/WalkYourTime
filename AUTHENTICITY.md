## What I Found in Imitation Apps

After reverse-engineering and thoroughly inspecting the code of several apps that claim to offer the same functionality, I uncovered concerning practices that you should be aware of before installing them.

### Non-Native or Obsolete Code
Many imitations are built with outdated frameworks or non-native wrappers, leading to poor performance, high battery drain, and compatibility issues.

### False Privacy Claims
Despite advertising themselves as "privacy-friendly", these apps often collect and transmit your data in the background without your explicit knowledge.

### Instability & Bugs
The code quality is frequently subpar, resulting in crashes, unreliable step tracking, and an overall frustrating user experience.

### Closed Source
None of these alternatives are open source. You cannot verify what they are actually doing with your data, forcing you to blindly trust them.

### Forced Daily Logins
These apps force you to log in every single day, nullifying the results and defeating the actual purpose of the app.
